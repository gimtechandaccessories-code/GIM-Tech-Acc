What we do

Two things, done properly

No trying to be everything. Repairs and sales — each handled to the same standard, wherever in South Africa you're calling from.

Selective

Laptop repair

Screens, hinges, keyboards, and storage upgrades, plus the diagnostics to find a no-power or no-display fault before we touch anything. We're selective — if a job isn't worth doing, we'll tell you honestly instead of taking it on.

Warranty + charger

Refurbished & new laptops

Mostly refurbished, tested and cleaned before they reach the shelf — with the occasional new laptop in stock too. Every laptop comes with a warranty and a charger included. We stock accessories as well.

All of SA

Nationwide shipping

Not in Durban? It doesn't matter. We ship anywhere in South Africa, mostly through The Courier Guy, so getting your laptop fixed — or finding your next one — doesn't depend on where you live.

Repair scope

What we fix

  • Screen replacement
  • Hinge repair
  • Keyboard replacement
  • Storage upgrades (HDD to SSD)
  • Laptop not turning on / no display
  • Motherboard-related issues

What we don't fix

We don't do CPU repair or removal. If a job needs that kind of chip-level work, we'll say so upfront rather than take your money for something we can't properly finish. We'd rather stay selective and get the jobs we do take right.

Typical turnaround: 1–5 working days, depending on the fault and part availability
